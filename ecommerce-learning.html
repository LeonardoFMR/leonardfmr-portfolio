01

Foundations

Inbound strategy & e-commerce KPIs

Inbound e-commerce focuses on attracting people through valuable content and experiences that respond to their needs and changing behavior. The methodology is described in three connected stages:

01

Attract

Help potential customers discover relevant products and content.

02

Engage

Reduce purchasing friction and support the decision process.

03

Delight

Provide a positive post-purchase experience that builds loyalty.

Metrics to follow

  • Website traffic
  • Conversion rate
  • Average order value (AOV)
  • Customer lifetime value (CLV)
  • Online revenue
02

Audience

Understanding your buyers

A buyer persona is a representation of an ideal customer informed by real data and informed assumptions. The course notes identify characteristics such as age, gender, location or nationality, income, education and technical ability.

RFM segmentation

Customer purchasing behavior can be examined through Recency, Frequency and Monetary value: How recently did someone buy? How often do they purchase? How much do they spend?

Buying modalities

Shorter cycles

Competitive

Logic-driven buyers seeking the best available option.

Shorter cycles

Spontaneous

Motivated by convenience and emotion; may justify decisions afterward.

Longer cycles

Methodical

Research carefully and rely on logic.

Longer cycles

Humanistic

Value personal connection, relationships and emotional relevance.

The notes distinguish ideal buyer profiles, which contribute quantitative information, from buying modalities, which help interpret purchase intent.

03

Execution planning

Planning & marketing automation

Plan offers around key milestones in the business year, then identify repetitive customer communications suitable for automated workflows.

Abandoned cart

Send a follow-up message when a customer leaves without completing checkout.

Post-purchase

Follow up on the buying experience and invite feedback.

Birthday campaigns

Use relevant occasions to reconnect with customers.

Win-back campaigns

Re-engage customers who have become inactive.

04

Inbound / Stage 1

Attract visitors

Product-page SEO checklist from the course

  • Give each product its own page and a descriptive URL built around a target keyword.
  • Use descriptive page titles with the product, category and website name.
  • Write unique meta descriptions and a meaningful H1; organize benefits, features, specifications and reviews with subheadings.
  • Add descriptive text to product images and consider structured product information such as pricing and reviews.

The original notes also record suggested limits of 125 characters for URLs and 300 for meta descriptions. These are course-note guidelines, not guaranteed search-engine requirements.

Additional acquisition channels

Blogging & seasonal content

Share useful expertise, shopping guides and a clear path toward products.

User-generated content

Ratings and reviews can help shoppers evaluate a purchase and provide fresh content.

Social media

Develop flexible brand content guidelines for consistent communication.

Paid media

Explore search ads, display ads, social ads and dynamic product retargeting.

05

Inbound / Stage 2

Engage & improve conversion

Catalog optimization means looking at what sells, what does not, and where the purchasing journey creates friction. The notes organize products by traffic and conversion:

Low traffic / High conversionPromote

Help more shoppers discover these products.

High traffic / High conversionTop sellers

Maintain visibility for proven performers.

Low traffic / Low conversionReview or remove

Evaluate whether the product deserves a place in the catalog.

High traffic / Low conversionOptimize

Investigate why visitors are not purchasing.

Other conversion tools from the notes

  • Welcome offers and popups: invite visitors to stay connected.
  • A/B testing: compare versions of a page or email to see which performs better.
  • Site search: compare searched products, clicked products and actual purchases.
  • Customer contact: provide a clear way for visitors to ask questions.
  • Merchandising: explore offers such as free shipping thresholds.
  • Cart recovery and remarketing: follow up through automated messages and targeted audiences.
06

Inbound / Stage 3

Delight & retain customers

A strong customer experience continues after checkout. The course emphasizes fulfillment, order tracking and small personalized touches.

Follow-up

Ask for product reviews, suggest complementary purchases and share relevant discounts.

Loyalty

Consider loyalty programs based on purchase tiers, and periodically review inactive email contacts.

07

Platform concepts

HubSpot + Shopify integration

My course notes describe how connecting an e-commerce store with a CRM can bring together product, customer and order information for marketing.

Data synchronization

Products, prices, customer records and orders can become available in the CRM. The notes describe matching customers using email addresses and linking orders to contacts when possible.

Segmentation

Use order and line-item data—such as purchases, items and quantities—and customer tags to define audiences.

Workflows & templates

The material covers welcome messages, abandoned-cart recovery and re-engagement emails.

Reports & dashboards

Use e-commerce reporting to analyze revenue, sales and cart recovery.

These are notes from the course, not a tested integration tutorial. Available integrations, workflows and subscription requirements may have changed since the material was recorded.
